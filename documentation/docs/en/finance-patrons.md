# Patrons

While the hands‑on litter‑picking happens outdoors, the coordination, data‑management, and outreach happen behind a screen. Nevertheless, we rely on generous supporters who believe in our mission and want to help us keep the project running smoothly.

We truly appreciate anyone who backs the initiative out of personal interest.

If you’d like to become a patron, please get in touch with one of our senior community members. We’ll discuss the kind of contribution you’d like to make—whether it’s a one‑off donation, recurring support, or sponsoring a specific upcoming expense. From the community’s standpoint, these contributions go directly toward covering operational costs, so every pledge makes a tangible difference.

Thank you for considering a partnership with us—your support helps keep our streets cleaner and our community thriving!

## Examples

To give you a clearer picture of how your support can make an immediate impact, here are a few concrete ways patrons have helped fund the project’s essential expenses. Any contribution—big or small—directly sustains the operations that keep our community clean and connected.


  - **Certificate renewal** – Our annual security certificate costs about **$15**. Sponsoring this fee keeps our site trusted and encrypted.

  - **Unused cloud credits** – If you have AWS (or another cloud provider) credits that will expire unused, you can donate them to cover hosting or compute expenses.

  - **Photo‑storage fees** – Storing the images we collect from litter‑picking events incurs ongoing storage costs. A small contribution toward this helps keep the gallery online. This is about **$5** a month.

  - **Server operating costs** – Each of our servers has modest monthly expenses. Even a modest monthly sponsorship goes a long way toward keeping the platform stable.

Any amount, big or small, is greatly appreciated and directly supports the day‑to‑day operation of the project. Thank you for helping us keep the environment clean!
