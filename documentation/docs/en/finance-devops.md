# DevOps Team

While the litter pickers handle the outdoor clean‑up work, the developers and operations volunteers keep everything running from behind their keyboards.

## Who We Are

Our DevOps crew is made up of enthusiastic volunteers who:

 - Develop new features – turning ideas into functional tools that help the project grow.
 - Fix bugs – hunting down and squashing issues across all of the codebases we rely on.
 - Maintain infrastructure – ensuring servers, pipelines, and monitoring stay healthy and secure.

## How You Can Help

If you’d like to contribute your skills, reach out to the community coordinators. They’ll:

  1. Provide the necessary repository access.
  2. Match you with a suitable project or issue.
  3. Offer guidance and support throughout your contribution.

## Operations Philosophy

We follow a NoOps mindset: most routine tasks are automated via services, scheduled jobs, and CI/CD pipelines. When a manual intervention is required, the DevOps team steps in promptly to resolve it.
